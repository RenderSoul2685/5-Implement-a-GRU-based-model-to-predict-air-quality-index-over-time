## 5. Aggregated Real Delhi Datasets (2023–2025)

### `delhi_stations_2023_2025.csv`
- **Location**: 6 Delhi Stations (`Anand_Vihar`, `Bawana`, `ITO`, `Mandir_Marg`, `Punjabi_Bagh`, `RK_Puram`)
